## ★★★ 1.1 Formulas

$$!H(X)=-\sum_xp(x)\log p(x)$$

*Meaning:* ==entropy== is the average surprisal.

## ★★★ 1.2 Statements to judge (one line each)

- ==Entropy== is concave.
- $H(X)\le\log|\mathcal X|$.
- $H(X)=0$ iff deterministic.

## ★★★ 1.3 2024 Q1 — example (6 marks)

*Q.* $X$ uniform on two symbols.

- (a) $H(X)=1$ bit.

> **Key** — the uniform distribution maximises entropy.
