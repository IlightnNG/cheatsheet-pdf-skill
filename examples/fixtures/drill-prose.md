## ★★★ 1.3 Worked example — entropy, conceptually

*Scenario.* Think of a skewed coin and how surprising each outcome is.

- The entropy measures the average surprisal.
- A fair coin is more uncertain than a biased one.
- Conditioning on side information can only reduce it.
